## Signature interaction

Isolate-a-criterion on the rubric: selecting one weight isolates it and dims the other three, pointer, keyboard and touch reachable, reversible, and inert under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Ticker

Persistent bottom bar, all routes. Static case-flavoured indicators, no API. Visibly and permanently labelled as sample case data so it can never be mistaken for live market data. Scroll stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`; pauses on hover and focus; content available as a static list to assistive technology; reserves its height rather than overlaying the primary action on mobile.

## Boundaries

Preserved: `@theme` tokens, Wordmark and TaglineRule, the markets language, `site.ts` as the single copy source, PRODUCT.md product truth.

Anti-goals: no new visual world, no photography-dependent composition, no invented proof, no gating the page behind a fork or a reveal, no kicker/eyebrow labels, no card grid as page structure.

## Content rules

Fabricated *history* is purged rather than placeheld: "Fourth edition", "First held 2023", "Alumni 640" and "last year's report" contradict confirmed first-edition truth. The form endpoint is deliberately unset and centralised in one constant.
